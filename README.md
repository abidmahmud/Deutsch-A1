# Netzwerk neu A1 · Deutsch–বাংলা

When I started learning German, most word lists I found explained things in English, and I kept translating twice in my head: German to English to Bangla. So I made my own glossary for the Netzwerk neu A1 book, with every word explained directly in Bangla and its pronunciation written in Bangla letters.

It started as a simple list and slowly grew into a small study app that I use every day on my tablet.

## What's inside

- **1,875 words** from all 12 chapters, each with a Bangla meaning, pronunciation in Bangla script and an example sentence
- **Flashcards that remember you.** Words you know come back less often, and words you miss come back sooner.
- **Quizzes:** meaning, pictures, der/die/das, plurals, verb forms, spelling and listening
- **49 short reading texts.** Tap any word to see what it means.
- **Grammar and pronunciation guides** in simple language, with Bangla explanations
- **A colour code for der · die · das:** blue ▲ for *der*, red ● for *die*, green ■ for *das*, plus the word endings that give the article away (Rechn**ung** → die)
- **Pictures** for most words, so you remember the thing and not just the translation
- **Stylus notes** on every page, for scribbling, underlining or drawing your own memory tricks
- **Progress tracking** with a daily streak, so you can see how far you've come

## How to use it

Open https://abidmahmud.github.io/Deutsch-A1/ in Chrome and tap **Install app** in the menu. After that it opens from the home screen like any other app and works without internet. Only the real voice recordings need a connection.

Your progress stays on your device. To move it to a new phone or tablet, go to **Fortschritt → Daten exportieren** and import the file on the other device.

## A small note

This is a personal study aid I made for myself and anyone else learning German from Bangla. It isn't connected to Klett or the official Netzwerk neu course, so please use it alongside the book, not instead of it. The Bangla pronunciation is an approximation to help beginners get started; listening to real German is still the best teacher.
